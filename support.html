Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to the most common queries. Can't find yours? Submit a request below.

How do I create a Saksham account?
Click Sign up on the homepage, choose your role (Trainee or Trainer), and complete the registration form via the Clerk authentication widget. Trainee accounts are activated instantly; Trainer accounts require administrator approval.
I forgot my password — how do I reset it?
On the Log in page, click "Forgot password?" in the Clerk sign-in widget. You'll receive a password-reset link on your registered email. If you don't see it, check your spam folder or contact support.
How do I enroll in a course?
Log in as a Trainee, go to the Courses page from your sidebar, browse available courses, and click Enroll. The course will appear on your dashboard and you can start learning immediately.
My trainer account is "pending approval" — what do I do?
Trainer accounts are manually reviewed by an administrator to verify credentials. This typically takes 1–2 working days. If you've waited longer, please submit a support request using the form below or email us directly.
How do assessments work?
Assessments are MCQ-based questionnaires set by your trainer, each with a deadline. Go to Assessments in your trainee dashboard, select the active assessment, and answer all questions within the time limit. Scores are recorded automatically.
Can I download my certificate?
Once you've completed all course requirements and passed the assessment, your certificate is auto-generated and available on your Profile page under the Certificates section. You can download it as a PDF.
How do I upload training material as a Trainer?
Go to your Trainer Library from the sidebar, click Upload Resource, select the file type (video, PDF, presentation), fill in the metadata, and upload. Materials are immediately available to enrolled trainees.
The site shows "sample data" instead of real content
This means the backend server is either not running or unreachable. The frontend automatically falls back to demo data so you can still explore the UI. To connect to real data, ensure the backend is running on the expected port (see SETUP.md).
