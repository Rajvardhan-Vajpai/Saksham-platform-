Built around three roles

Every account is approved before it becomes active, and each role sees a workspace shaped for its work.

Trainee

Build a professional profile and take charge of your own learning path.

  • Manage qualifications, experience, skills and certificates
  • Enroll in courses and track progress
  • Attempt subject-wise MCQ assessments
  • Share feedback on courses and trainers

Trainer

Design assessments and keep a library of learning material for your trainees.

  • Create MCQ questionnaires with deadlines
  • Upload recorded lectures, slides and reading material
  • Monitor trainee participation and scores
  • Maintain a shared Trainer Library

Admin

Oversee the programme end to end, from approvals to competency planning.

  • Approve accounts and manage roles
  • Monitor courses, enrollments and certifications
  • Map trainer competencies to subjects
  • Publish homepage notices and achievements
